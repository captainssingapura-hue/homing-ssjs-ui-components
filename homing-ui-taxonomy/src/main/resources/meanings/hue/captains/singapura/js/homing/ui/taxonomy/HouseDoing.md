# Doing - what a part lets the user do to its owner

## Doing

A part that lets the user act on its owner: pick, decide, change, look, manage, go. It is how
the user's intent reaches the owner.

## Choosing

Offers the user something to pick.

## Choice

One thing offered to pick among others; picking it is the whole of its use.

## Style

Offers a design style for the theme - how things are drawn, apart from their colours.

## Palette

Offers a palette for the theme - the colours a style is worn in.

## What

Asks what kind of thing to open.

## How

Asks how the opened thing should arrive.

## Where

Asks where the opened thing should go.

## Picker

Asks the user which kind of thing to open in the owner, when the owner has nothing to show.

## Query

Takes what the user types to narrow the owner's choices to those that match: the search of a
combobox or of a command palette.

## Committing

Ends a decision the owner put to the user.

## Go

Does what the user chose, ending the choosing.

## Cancel

Leaves the decision without doing anything.

## Action

One of the actions the owner offers, done when the user commits to it.

## Skip

Passes the step the owner is at, choosing nothing, and moves on: a question left unanswered.

## Changing

Changes the owner's value.

## Toggle

Flips the owner's setting between on and off.

## Adjust

Sets the owner's number anywhere along its scale.

## Thumb

Takes the user's hand, so the owner's value moves with it.

## Splitter

Takes the user's hand at the boundary between two of the owner's regions, so the room moves from the one to the other
with it: what makes a split resizable.

## Reset

Puts the owner's value back to its default, undoing what the user set.

## Increase

Raises the owner's value by one step: the plus beside a number.

## Decrease

Lowers the owner's value by one step: the minus beside a number.

## Clear

Empties what the user gave the owner, leaving it as though nothing had been given: the cross
inside a field. Not its default - nothing at all.

## Viewing

Changes how the owner is seen, never what it holds.

## ZoomIn

Brings the owner's view closer, to see more detail.

## ZoomOut

Takes the owner's view further away, to see more of it.

## Fit

Fits the whole of the owner's picture into view.

## Managing

Changes what the owner holds, or whether it stays.

## Close

Closes the owner, or what it stands for; it is gone until opened again.

## Add

Adds one more member to the owner.

## Remove

Takes the owner out of what holds it, or one of its things away: the cross on a tag, the remove
of an attachment.

## Going

Takes the user from the owner to somewhere else.

## Open

Goes to the thing the owner stands for.

## Home

Goes to the site's home, from anywhere in it.

## Preferences

Gives the user, wherever the owner frames a page, the way to their preferences for the whole site.

## Path

Shows the way back from where the user is, step by step, and takes them along it.

## Step

One step of the way back, taking the user there.

## Previous

Goes to the one before, among things in order: the previous page, month, slide or question.

## Next

Goes to the one after, among things in order: the next page, month, slide or question.

## First

Goes to the first of things in order, however far back: the first page of the results.

## Last

Goes to the last of things in order, however far on: the last page of the results.

## More

Shows the steps the owner has folded away to save room: the ellipsis of a trail, or of a run of
pages.
