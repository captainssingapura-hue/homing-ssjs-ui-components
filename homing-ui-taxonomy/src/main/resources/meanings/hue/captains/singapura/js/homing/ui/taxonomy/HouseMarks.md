# The house's marks and tracks

## Separator

Rules one group of rows off from the next, across the list or the menu that holds them.

## BoundaryLine

Where two regions of a layout meet: the line between them, the layout's own, drawn as thick as its look says or not
drawn at all.

## Delimiter

Marks where one step of a run ends and the next begins, on the same line as the steps.

## Lamp

Says by a light how something is: alive, dormant, broken.

## BrandMark

The brand's own sign beside its name: a square of its colour, until the site gives a logo.

## Indicator

A small sign of a state: which one is current, which one has the keys.

## DropMark

Shows where a dragged thing would land.

## ToggleGroove

The pill a switch's knob slides in, showing by its colour that the switch is on.
