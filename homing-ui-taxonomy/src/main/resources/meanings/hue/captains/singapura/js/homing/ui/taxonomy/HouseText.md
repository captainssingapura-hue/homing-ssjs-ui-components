# The house's text

## Label

Words that name a control or a field, so the user knows what using it will touch.

## Heading1

A page's own name: the first heading of its outline, read before anything else on it.

## Heading2

A part of the page: the heading of one of its great divisions.

## Heading3

A section within a part, under its heading.

## Heading4

A smaller section, or what a container holds - a card's, a group's, a setting's title.

## Heading5

A division within a smaller section.

## Heading6

The least heading: a division within that.

## Kicker

A few words above a heading that say where it belongs.

## Caption

Quiet words beside something, adding to it without competing with it.

## Lede

The first words of a body, saying what follows in brief.

## Readout

A value read out exactly, as it stands now.

## Badge

A short word that sorts or flags what it is beside.

## Pill

A count, kept short, of what cannot all be seen.

## Wordmark

A name set as the mark of whom it belongs to.

## Code

Shows words that are code - a name, a value, a command - as code, so they are read as written.

## SuccessBadge

Says in a word that a thing went well: done, passed, resolved.

## WarningBadge

Says in a word that a thing needs a look: in progress, pending, unplaced.

## DangerBadge

Says in a word that a thing went wrong: failed, blocked, broken.

## Strong

Words said with weight among the words round them, otherwise as those words are.

## Muted

Words said under the words round them, in the faded ink, otherwise as those words are.
