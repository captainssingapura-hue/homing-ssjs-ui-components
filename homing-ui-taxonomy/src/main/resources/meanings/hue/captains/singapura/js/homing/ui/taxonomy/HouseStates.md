# The house's tree of states

## Availability

Whether the user can use the component, and how far: at all, only to read it, or not yet, because it
is still at work.

## Picking

Whether the component is chosen, and how: pressed on, marked, picked from among others, or - for one
that speaks for a group - how much of the group is.

## Disclosure

Whether what the component opens is open, or whether the component is itself on view over the page
for the time being.

## Validity

Whether what the component holds will do: whether it must be given, and whether what was given is
refused.

## Place

Whether the component is where the user is, is what the user is about to pick, or is picked out by
something other than the user's hand.

## Content

Whether the component holds anything yet.

## Interaction

What the user's hand and keys are doing with the component at this moment: over it, pressing it,
focused on it, or holding the page's keys in it.

## Physical

Where the component sits in the depth of the page, as its holder decides.

## Counting

How often a thing was done to the component: how many times it was clicked.

## Given

What the user has given the component: the words typed into it, the option chosen of those it
offers.

## Enabled

Whether the user can use the component at all. True at rest; false, it takes no press and no keys,
and says that it would not, so nobody tries in vain.

## ReadOnly

The user can read the component's value, reach it and copy it, but not change it: a value shown for
reference, where a field would otherwise be.

## Busy

The component is at work: what it shows, or what it was asked to do, is on the way, and it says so
rather than looking finished or broken.

## Pressed

A button that stays pressed until it is pressed again: a toggle that is on, such as bold in an
editor's bar.

## Checked

Chosen by a mark of its own: a box ticked, a radio filled, a switch turned on, an item of a menu with
its check. Yes or no, and nothing between.

## Coverage

How much of the group the component speaks for is chosen: none, some or all - the checkbox at the
head of a table's rows or of a tree's children. Only such a checkbox has it; a plain one is checked or
not.

## Selected

Picked from among others of its kind for what is done next: an option of a list, a tab of a strip, a
day of a calendar, a row of a table. One or several may be, as their holder allows.

## Expanded

What the component opens is open: an accordion's section, the body of a disclosure, the list a
select drops, a sidebar unfolded. Said on what opens it, so the user knows before they press.

## Shown

The component is on view over the page for the time being: a dialog, a popover, a tooltip, a menu.
Said on what is opened, as Expanded is said on what opens it.

## Required

The user must give the component's value before what it belongs to can be sent.

## Invalid

What the component holds is refused: the user must change it, and is told why.

## Current

The one of its kind that is where the user is now: the step of a trail that is this page, the page
among the pages, today among the days.

## Highlighted

Picked out from its kind by something other than the user's hand on it: a search's hit, the rows a
chart points at.

## Pointed

The one among its kind that the user's keys or pointer are on, not yet chosen: the option that Enter
would pick in a list, a menu or a combobox.

## Empty

The component holds nothing yet: a field with nothing typed, a list with no rows, a pane with no
tabs.

## Hovered

The pointer is over the component, and the user may be about to press it.

## Focused

The browser's focus is on the component and is shown, because the user reached it with the keys and
must see where they are.

## Down

The user's hand is down on the component and not yet let go: what pressing it does has not happened.

## Keyboard

Where the page's keys are, as the keyboard's steward keeps them: away; a candidate, where the keys
would come if the user confirmed; lent, here and handed to a control inside; or held, here and
listened to.

## Dragging

Held by the user's hand and being moved, from the press that grabbed it until it is let go.

## Held

Held down by the user's hand, as the component has it: up at rest, down from its press until its
release. Unlike the browser's own down, it is the component's, and what holding it means is the
component's to say.

## Elevation

How far off its own plane the component sits: flat, lifted towards the user or pressed in, its holder
deciding what that says.

## Orientation

Which way a component laid along a line runs: across the page, or stood up, as a fader is.

## Clicks

How many times the component has been clicked, from none: a click is its going up. Nothing it shows
changes as it counts.

## DraftText

The words typed into the component so far, the user still at them: none at rest.

## CommittedText

The words the user is done typing, by Enter or by leaving the component: none at rest.

## ChosenOption

The option chosen of those the component offers, by its value: none at rest.
