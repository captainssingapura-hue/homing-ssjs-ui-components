# Shaping - how a part makes up its owner's room

## Shaping

A part that makes up its owner's room: its bands, its layers, what it shows, its members, the
lines between them, and what the hand takes hold of.

## Bands

Strips of the owner's room, each with a job of its own.

## Head

The band at the owner's top that names it and carries its controls.

## Actions

The band of the owner's actions, where a decision is ended.

## Tabs

The band of the owner's tabs, through which the user moves between what it holds.

## Layers

What the owner shows over or under the page, apart from the flow of it.

## Window

The window the owner shows itself in, above the page.

## Veil

Veils what is behind the owner, so the user attends to the owner alone.

## Float

A pane floating over the owner, free of its layout.

## Popup

A menu shown where it was asked for, for as long as the user needs it.

## Contents

What the owner shows.

## Page

What the owner's window shows: the reason the window was opened.

## Host

Holds the one tab the owner carries.

## Index

The list the user picks from, beside what the pick shows.

## Detail

What the user's pick shows, beside the list it was picked from.

## Specimen

A menu shown still, to be looked at rather than used.

## Setting

The field a setting is presented on: what it is called, a note on it, and the way back to its
default.

## Layout

Lays the owner's regions out, and keeps them laid out as they change.

## Members

One of many the owner holds alike.

## Member

One of the controls the owner gathers under one heading.

## Entry

One of the things the owner lists, shown rather than picked.

## Dock

One tabbed pane, in one region of the owner.

## Cell

One region of a grid, drawn to show its place.

## Dividers

What separates the owner's regions or members.

## Seam

The line between two regions, moved by hand to give one more room.

## Break

Marks where a group or a step of the owner ends and the next begins.

## Handles

What the hand or the pointer takes hold of.

## Reveal

Brings the owner into view when the pointer reaches the edge it waits at.

## Chip

Carries the owner into a strip, where it is picked, moved and closed.
